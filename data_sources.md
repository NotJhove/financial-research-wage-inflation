# Data Sources

## Consumer Prices

- Source: FRED
- Series: Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers: All Items in U.S. City Average
- Series ID: CPIAUCSL
- Description: Measures the average change over time in prices paid by urban consumers for a broad basket of goods and services.
- Frequency: Monthly
- Unit: Index
- Source URL: https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/CPIAUCSL

## Wages

- Source: FRED
- Series: Average Hourly Earnings of Production and Nonsupervisory Employees, Total Private
- Series ID: AHETPI
- Description: Measures average hourly earnings for production and nonsupervisory employees in the private sector.
- Frequency: Monthly
- Unit: Dollars per hour
- Source URL: https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/AHETPI
