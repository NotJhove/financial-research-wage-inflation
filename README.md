# Did Wages Keep Up With Inflation?

## Research Question

Did wage growth keep up with inflation in the United States after 2020?

## Hypothesis

Although US wages increased after 2020, inflation temporarily grew faster than wages, reducing workers' purchasing power.

## Objective

Use publicly available economic data from the Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED) database to investigate the relationship between inflation, wage growth, and real purchasing power.

## Planned Analysis

1. Examine changes in consumer prices.
2. Compare inflation with wage growth.
3. Measure the effect on workers' purchasing power.
4. Present the findings as a three-chart visual story.

## Data Source

Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis — FRED
